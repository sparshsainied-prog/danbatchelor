Dan

I'm a primary school teacher, and I was diagnosed with dyslexia late. I wanted to show my pupils that anything is possible.

So I made Jack Palmer a relatable, engaging hero in a story that's a playful, fun way into science fiction while staying rooted in reality, with morality, power and friendship at its heart.
